Demo campaign · AR Branding Platform

The poster is the landing page.

NOVA MIND is a fictional AI study-buddy app launching at TechFest '26. This is a working demo of the platform: print the poster, scan the QR, point your camera at the artwork, and the campaign comes alive — no app download. Every scan, start, and tap is measured per placement.

Try the flow

1. Open the poster and print it (or show it on a second screen).
2. Scan the poster's QR code with your phone — or open the experience directly.
3. Allow camera access and point at the poster artwork. The mascot locks onto it.
4. Tap the call to action, then check the dashboard.

Honest labels: the AR page is real image-target tracking (MindAR + Three.js). The fallback is a 3D viewer, not AR, and the site says so. Analytics on this static demo are stored on this device only — the production platform uses the server event pipeline.

The campaign artwork (the AR target)

This artwork is the tracked target in this demo build. In the production studio, a brand compiles its own poster or packaging as the target.
