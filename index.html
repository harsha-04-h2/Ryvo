01 — How the campaign runsFour steps, zero installs
STEP 1

Print & place

The poster goes up at the main gate. Its artwork is the AR target — compiled, not decorative.

STEP 2

Scan the code

The QR opens the experience in the browser and tags the session to this placement: p=poster.

STEP 3

Point at the art

The camera finds the artwork and the mascot locks onto it in 3D. That lock-on is the campaign moment.

STEP 4

Count everything

Scans, starts, lock-ons, CTA taps, leads — broken down by placement on the dashboard.
